# NuzLike alpha 6

## What changed

- Red, Blue, Yellow and Crystal now consistently enforce the selected level
  caps during experience calculation.
- Crystal no longer lowers an over-levelled Pokémon after a capped experience
  award. Its level and accumulated experience are preserved.
- Eggs in Crystal are no longer mistaken for fainted Pokémon and moved to the
  Memorial after battle.
- Crystal trainer battles no longer risk opening as an empty “But no one
  came…” encounter because of leftover encounter state.

Existing saves do not need to restart.

## Alpha warning

NuzLike is alpha software and not every game has been played through from start
to finish. Keep your original game backup untouched and use a separate save you
are prepared to lose. Bug reports are very welcome.
